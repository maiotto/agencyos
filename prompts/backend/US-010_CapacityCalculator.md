# US-010 – Capacity Calculator

Sprint: 7 – Capacity Foundation

Status: Ready for Implementation

Priority: High

---

# Objective

Implement the Capacity Calculator Service.

The service calculates the operational capacity of every active Execution Resource.

Capacity represents the amount of productive work available during a defined planning period.

---

# Business Value

Capacity is the primary operational indicator used by AgencyOS.

Every future recommendation generated by the Decision Engine depends on accurate capacity calculations.

---

# User Story

As an Operations Manager,

I want to calculate available operational capacity,

So that I understand how much execution capability exists before accepting or planning new work.

---

# Inputs

Planning Period

Execution Resource

Assignments

Working Calendar

Resource Capacity

---

# Outputs

For every Execution Resource return:

- Total Capacity
- Allocated Hours
- Available Hours
- Utilization Percentage
- Remaining Capacity

---

# Formula

Available Capacity

=

Total Capacity

-

Allocated Hours

---

# Business Rules

BR-001

Only Active Resources are calculated.

BR-002

Cancelled Assignments are ignored.

BR-003

Completed Assignments are ignored.

BR-004

Planning period is mandatory.

BR-005

Capacity cannot become negative.
