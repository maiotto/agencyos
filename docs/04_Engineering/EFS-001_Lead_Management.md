# 3. Business Context

Lead Management represents the first business capability of AgencyOS.

Every commercial opportunity starts as a Lead.

A Lead represents a potential customer before becoming a Client.

A Lead is never operational.

A Lead never creates Missions.

A Lead never creates Contracts.

Client creation in MVP 1.0 occurs only through Lead conversion.

Lead Conversion Model is Option B:

- Conversion creates exactly one Client Aggregate Root
- The Lead Aggregate Root is preserved
- Lead status becomes Converted
- The Lead entity is never transformed into a Client entity
- Lead history remains available

# 11. Business Rules

Authoritative Business Rules are defined in Business_Rules.md.

This Engineering Specification implements the following Commercial Business Rules for Lead Management.

## BR-COM-001 — Lead Lifecycle Status

Every Lead shall have exactly one valid lifecycle status at all times.

Implemented statuses:

New

Qualified

Proposal

Negotiation

Won

Lost

Converted

Archived

---

## BR-COM-002 — Lead Conversion

Conversion is explicit and never automatic.

Only a Lead in status Won may be converted.

Conversion creates exactly one Client.

The Lead remains a Lead Aggregate Root with status Converted.

Lead history is preserved.

Converted Leads are read-only for business workflows.

---

## BR-COM-003 — Client Creation and Mandatory Information

Clients originate from Lead conversion.

Direct Client creation without Lead conversion is not allowed in MVP 1.0.

Conversion shall supply mandatory Client identification:

LegalName

TaxIdentifier

---

## BR-COM-010 — Commercial Audit History

Lead Aggregate Root shall generate Audit Events for successful state-changing operations.

GetLeadHistoryQuery obtains history from centralized AuditEvents.

---

## BR-COM-012 — Commercial Aggregate Ownership

Lead is a Commercial Aggregate Root.

Contact, Contract and Mission are outside the Lead Aggregate.

---

## BR-COM-013 — Archive Policy

Archive is the business inactivation model for Lead.

Archived Leads have Status = Archived and ArchivedAt populated.

Archived Leads are read-only.

SoftDelete is not part of Lead business workflows.

---

## BR-COM-014 — Closed Enumerations

LeadStatus, LeadSource, CompanySize and Industry follow ADR-012.

---

## Engineering Transition Constraints

The following constraints implement BR-COM-001 and BR-COM-002 inside the Lead state machine.

Only New Leads may become Qualified.

Only Qualified Leads may enter Proposal.

Only Proposal Leads may enter Negotiation.

Only Negotiation Leads may become Won.

Only Won Leads may be Converted.

Lost Leads cannot be converted.

Archived Leads cannot be converted.

Converted Leads cannot return to an editable status.

Any non-Converted Lead may transition to Lost where the state machine allows.

Any non-Converted Lead may transition to Archived where the state machine allows.

Every successful state transition shall be auditable according to BR-COM-010 and ADR-009.

Application orchestration across Aggregate Roots

# 46. Architecture Compliance

## ADR-009

Lead produces Domain Events and Audit Events after successful commit.

GetLeadHistoryQuery reads centralized AuditEvents only.

Event Sourcing is not used.

## ADR-010

Lead is an Aggregate Root.

Conversion creates Client outside the Lead Aggregate.

Transactional boundaries remain Aggregate-aligned.

## ADR-011

Archive is the business inactivation model.

SoftDelete is technical only.

## ADR-012

Lead enumerations are C# enums persisted as smallint.

No lookup tables.

No enumeration seed data.

API and Swagger use enum names.
