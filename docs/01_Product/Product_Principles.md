# AgencyOS Product Scope

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official scope of the AgencyOS Product.

Its purpose is to establish clear functional boundaries for the MVP and future product evolution, preventing scope expansion during implementation.

This document complements the Product Vision and the Program Baseline.

---

# Product Scope

AgencyOS is an AI-First Goal-Oriented Operational Planning Platform for service organizations.

The product supports organizations in transforming business goals into optimized operational execution strategies.

AgencyOS focuses on operational decision making before execution begins.

# Product Boundaries

AgencyOS is responsible for:

- Operational Planning
- Capacity Planning
- Decision Support
- Operational Intelligence

AgencyOS is not responsible for:

- Financial Accounting
- Payroll
- CRM Marketing
- ERP Functions
- Human Resources
- Legal Compliance
- Customer Relationship Automation

Integration with these systems may occur in future releases.

---

# Product Success Criteria

AgencyOS achieves its objectives when organizations can:

Understand operational capacity.

Evaluate execution alternatives.

Identify operational constraints.

Support commercial decisions using measurable data.

Reduce operational risk.

Improve resource utilization.

Produce explainable operational recommendations.

Planning quality is considered a primary product success indicator.

# Official Scope Statement

AgencyOS is an AI-First Goal-Oriented Operational Planning Platform focused on operational planning, capacity management and explainable decision support for service organizations.

The product intentionally excludes traditional ERP and CRM capabilities, concentrating instead on optimizing operational decision making before execution begins.
