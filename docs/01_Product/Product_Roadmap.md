# AgencyOS Product Roadmap

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term evolution of the AgencyOS Product.

Unlike the Sprint Register, which records completed work, the Product Roadmap describes the planned evolution of the product beyond the current baseline.

The roadmap represents strategic direction rather than implementation commitments.

---

# Product Evolution

AgencyOS evolves through progressive maturity stages.

Each stage extends the previous one while preserving the architectural principles established by the Product Vision, Product Principles and Program Architecture.

```
Operational Management
        ↓
Operational Planning
        ↓
Operational Intelligence
        ↓
Decision Support
        ↓
Decision Intelligence
        ↓
Goal-Oriented Operational Planning
```

# Product Evolution Principles

Every release shall preserve the following principles:

Business First

Capacity First

Goal-Oriented Planning

Deterministic Core

Explainable Decisions

Human Governance

Documentation First

Incremental Evolution

No future capability may violate these principles.

# Official Roadmap Statement

AgencyOS evolves incrementally from an operational planning platform into a comprehensive AI-First Goal-Oriented Operational Planning Platform.

Each release extends the product while preserving its architectural integrity, deterministic business foundation and human-centered decision governance.
