# Vision

AgencyOS is an AI-First Goal-Oriented Operational Planning Platform designed for service businesses.

Its purpose is to transform business objectives into optimized operational execution by combining deterministic operational intelligence with Artificial Intelligence.

Rather than managing projects or tasks, AgencyOS assists organizations in making better operational decisions before execution begins.

# Product Positioning

AgencyOS is not an ERP.

AgencyOS is not a CRM.

AgencyOS is not a Project Management tool.

AgencyOS is an Operational Decision Platform.

Its primary responsibility is to recommend the best execution strategy for each business scenario.

---

# Problem Statement

Service companies frequently experience operational problems caused by decisions made without sufficient visibility into capacity and operational constraints.

Typical questions include:

- Can we accept another client?
- Do we have enough capacity?
- Should we hire?
- Should we outsource?
- Which team should execute this work?
- What happens if deadlines change?
- Which strategy produces the best business outcome?

These decisions are often based on intuition rather than measurable operational data.

AgencyOS addresses this gap by providing structured operational intelligence and decision support.

# Product Principles

The following principles guide every product decision.

## Capacity First

Capacity is the central operational asset.

Commercial decisions must consider available capacity.

---

## Goal-Oriented Planning

Planning begins with business objectives.

Resources become part of the solution.

---

## Deterministic Core

Business calculations remain deterministic.

Artificial Intelligence complements deterministic business logic.

---

## Explainable Decisions

Every recommendation must explain:

- Why it was selected.
- Which constraints influenced the result.
- Which alternatives were rejected.

---

## Human Governance

Artificial Intelligence proposes.

Humans approve.

Operational responsibility always remains with authorized users.

# Success Criteria

AgencyOS succeeds when organizations can answer operational questions using measurable data instead of intuition.

Success indicators include:

- Better resource utilization.
- Improved delivery predictability.
- Reduced operational risk.
- Higher planning quality.
- Explainable operational decisions.

---

# Relationship with the AI Factory

AgencyOS Product and AgencyOS AI Factory are independent programs.

AgencyOS Product defines business capabilities.

AgencyOS AI Factory accelerates engineering through specialized AI agents.

The AI Factory supports product delivery but does not define product strategy.
