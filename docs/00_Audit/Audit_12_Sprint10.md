# AgencyOS Program Audit

## Audit 12

**Sprint:** Sprint 10 - Decision Intelligence Engine

**Status:** Completed

---

# Objective

Redesign the strategic direction of AgencyOS by evolving the Decision Engine into a Decision Intelligence Engine capable of planning, optimizing and explaining operational strategies.

Sprint 10 focuses on product architecture and research rather than feature implementation.

---

# Summary

Sprint 10 marks the beginning of a new phase in the evolution of AgencyOS.

Until Sprint 9 the platform had reached a complete and validated MVP capable of managing the commercial flow, operational planning and deterministic decision support.

Sprint 10 shifts the focus from software implementation to product innovation.

The objective is no longer to evaluate existing execution strategies, but to design an intelligent planning engine capable of generating, optimizing and explaining operational plans.

This sprint is primarily architectural.

---

# Main Decisions

## Product Evolution

AgencyOS evolves from:

Operational Decision Support System

to

Goal-Oriented Operational Planning Platform.

Status:

Maintained

---

## Decision Intelligence Engine

The Decision Engine evolves into a higher-level architecture called Decision Intelligence Engine.

Main components:

- Constraint Engine
- Strategy Generation Engine
- Optimization Engine
- Ranking Engine
- Explainability Engine
- Simulation Engine

Status:

Maintained

---

## Goal-Oriented Planning

Planning should begin with business objectives rather than available resources.

Examples:

- Deliver 10 videos
- Budget limit
- Delivery deadline
- Required quality level

The platform should generate the operational strategy required to achieve the objective.

Status:

Maintained

---

## Constraint Engine

Business rules become reusable constraints.

Examples:

- Budget
- Deadline
- Skills
- Capacity
- Policies
- Human approval

Constraints become independent components instead of application logic.

Status:

Maintained

---

## Strategy Generation

The system should generate multiple feasible execution strategies instead of evaluating only predefined alternatives.

Status:

Maintained

---

## Optimization Engine

Optimization becomes an independent architectural component.

Potential optimization objectives include:

- Lowest Cost
- Shortest Delivery Time
- Lowest Risk
- Highest Margin
- Maximum AI Utilization
- Balanced Resource Allocation

Status:

Maintained

---

## Explainable Intelligence

The explanation engine evolves from explaining the selected strategy to explaining both accepted and rejected alternatives.

Decision transparency remains mandatory.

Status:

Maintained

---

## Simulation Engine

The platform should support "What-if" simulations.

Examples:

- Hire an additional editor
- Increase budget
- Reduce deadline
- Replace human resources with AI
- Change company policies

Status:

Maintained

---

## Deterministic + AI Hybrid Architecture

The architecture explicitly separates:

Deterministic Components

- Capacity
- Workload
- Availability
- Constraints
- Ranking

AI Components

- Strategy generation
- Scenario exploration
- Recommendation refinement

Status:

Maintained

---

## Research Before Implementation

Sprint 10 established that architecture design precedes implementation.

The sprint became an Architecture Design Sprint instead of a traditional development sprint.

Status:

Maintained

---

Sprint 10 introduces the highest architectural layer of AgencyOS.

The platform evolves to:

Business Domains

↓

Operational Layer

↓

Analytical Layer

Decision Engine

↓

Decision Intelligence Engine

This new layer becomes the strategic core of the platform.

# Decisions Later Refined

None identified.

---

# Decisions Discarded

The previous planning model centered on resource allocation was superseded.

Planning is now centered on business objectives.

Resources become part of the solution rather than the starting point.

---

# Documentation Gaps Identified

Future documentation should formally describe:

- Decision Intelligence Architecture
- Constraint Catalog
- Optimization Algorithms
- Simulation Framework
- Goal Model
- AI Integration Strategy

---

# Impact

Sprint 10 redefined the long-term positioning of AgencyOS.

The platform is no longer intended to be only an operational management system.

Its strategic objective becomes intelligent operational planning driven by goals, constraints and optimization.

This establishes a clear differentiation from traditional ERP, CRM and project management platforms.

---

# Audit Conclusion

Sprint 10 successfully established the long-term architectural vision of AgencyOS.

The MVP created in previous sprints remains valid.

Sprint 10 does not replace the MVP architecture.

Instead, it extends it by defining the next evolutionary layer of the platform.

The Decision Intelligence Engine becomes the strategic direction for all future development.
