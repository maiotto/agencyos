# AgencyOS Program Audit

## Audit 01

**Phase:** Phase 1 - Product Definition

**Status:** Completed

---

# Objective

Reconstruct the original product vision and identify which decisions remained valid, which evolved and which were discarded.

---

# Summary

Phase 1 established the conceptual foundation of AgencyOS.

The product was conceived as an operational system for small video and content agencies whose central differentiator would be Capacity Planning.

During this phase the conceptual architecture, business modules, operational flow and initial roadmap were defined.

No implementation decisions were made during this phase.

---

# Main Decisions

## Product Vision

AgencyOS is a SaaS platform focused on operational management for agencies.

Status:

Maintained

---

## Product Core

The center of the product is Capacity Planning.

Business decisions must be driven by productive capacity.

Status:

Maintained

---

## Unit of Measure

The operational unit is Hours.

The product is not based on videos or projects.

Every service consumes productive hours.

Status:

Maintained

---

The following conceptual modules were defined:

# Architecture Evolution

The initial architecture was organized by business modules.

Later, the project evolved into Domain Driven modules and specialized Engines.

This is considered an architectural evolution, not a change of product vision.

None identified.

# Documentation Gaps Identified

The following artifacts were identified as missing during Phase 1:

- Entity Relationship Diagram
- Data Dictionary

- Relational Model
- Complete Domain Model

---

# Impact

Phase 1 successfully established the business vision of AgencyOS.

The product vision remained consistent throughout all subsequent sprints.

Later phases expanded the architecture without changing the original business objectives.

---

# Audit Conclusion

Phase 1 is considered conceptually successful.

The product vision remained stable.

Subsequent work focused on expanding technical depth rather than redefining business direction.
