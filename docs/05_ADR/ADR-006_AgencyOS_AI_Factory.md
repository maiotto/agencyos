# ADR-006

Title

AgencyOS AI Factory

Status

Accepted

Date

2026-07-21

---

## Context

AgencyOS has reached the implementation phase.

During the engineering planning, it became evident that an AI-First software engineering platform could significantly accelerate software delivery without changing the AgencyOS product itself.

The platform is named AgencyOS AI Factory.

---

## Decision

AgencyOS AI Factory is established as an independent strategic program.

AgencyOS AI Factory is not part of the AgencyOS MVP.

The AI Factory evolves independently.

AgencyOS remains the product.

AI Factory remains the engineering platform.

---

## Vision

AI executes.

Humans govern.

Humans remain responsible for:

- Product Vision
- Strategy
- Governance
- Final Decisions

---

## Governance

Until the AgencyOS MVP is delivered,

the AI Factory shall not require changes to:

- Product Architecture
- Domain Model
- Product Roadmap
- Product Backlog
- Product Schedule

---

## Principle

AgencyOS AI Factory accelerates AgencyOS.

AgencyOS never slows down to accommodate AI Factory.

---

## Consequences

AgencyOS keeps implementation focus.

AI Factory evolves in parallel.

Both programs share vision but not delivery dependencies.
